De-Identification Services (DIS) Profile

Volume 1 Review Guidelines — ITI Face-to-Face, November 2026

Four read-and-comment sessions reviewing the DIS Profile Volume 1 (early draft, v0.0.1-current). Each session: 20 min reading, 10 min discussion. Volume 2 (transactions) is not yet drafted; these sessions cover Volume 1 only.

DIS defines a policy-governed de-identification workflow for HL7 FHIR health data with four actors, four transactions, and five options. It separates the mandatory HL7 FHIR R4 protocol binding (transaction APIs) from the payload binding (content model for data being de-identified, version- and format-independent).

Session 1: Actors, Transactions & Content Modules Mon Nov 9  |  9:30–10:00 AM CST (3:30–4:00 PM UTC)
Read: Introduction (top of page) through §1:PXX.1.2.4  |  ~2,000 words
DIS separates a mandatory HL7 FHIR R4 protocol binding (transaction APIs) from the payload binding (content model for the data being de-identified). The payload binding is version- and format-independent.
Four actors: Requester, De-ID Manager, De-Identifier, and an Integrated De-ID Manager/De-Identifier that internalizes ITI-x3/x7. The Integrated actor can operate standalone — ITI-x1 is Optional (O).
Session 2: Options & Required Groupings Tue Nov 10  |  8:30–9:00 AM CST (2:30–3:00 PM UTC)
Read: §1:PXX.2 through §1:PXX.3  |  ~2,000 words
Five options: DIS-FHIR-Payload (required in Phase 1, structured as option for future DICOM/CDA bindings), DIS-ASYNC-JOB and DIS-ASYNC-TASK (independent of each other), DIS-IRREVERSIBLE and DIS-REVERSIBLE (at least one required).
HL7 FHIR R5/R6 payloads flow through the R4 protocol via version-safe packaging and version-conditional path mapping at execution plan compilation time. Required groupings: ATNA for all actors, IUA for Manager and Integrated actor.
Session 3: Concepts — Policy, Conformance & Payload Framework Wed Nov 11  |  9:00–9:30 AM CST (3:00–3:30 PM UTC)
Read: §1:PXX.4.1 Concepts  |  ~1,700 words (dense — use extra time for discussion)
Policy model: Trust is anchored in the policy's digital signature. Four policy variants: inline policy, by-reference, registered policy identifier, and named execution plan. Registered policies are format-neutral and need compilation; named execution plans are format-specific and dispatched directly.
Two conformance layers: content conformance (output + evidence conform to a content module) is the foundation; transaction conformance layers on top when actors communicate over the wire. Phase 1 scope is record-level actions only — dataset-level statistical disclosure control is deferred.
Session 4: Use Cases Thu Nov 12  |  8:30–9:00 AM CST (2:30–3:00 PM UTC)
Read: §1:PXX.4.2 Use Cases (all five)  |  ~2,300 words
Five use cases spanning async cohort (UC-1, UC-2), sync single-patient (UC-3, UC-4), and standalone content-only export (UC-5). UC-2 illustrates cross-format consistency scope reuse (HL7 FHIR + future DICOM). UC-4 shows trust-boundary separation between hospital and cloud AI.
Open issue (UC-5): The standalone Integrated actor has no wire-testable transaction. The spec asks whether a Data Recipient actor and push-delivery transaction should be added to Phase 1.

Offline review (comments welcome via issue tracker): §1:PXX.5 Security Considerations (~750 words) and §1:PXX.6 Cross-Profile Considerations (~300 words) are not covered in the F2F sessions. These sections are available for asynchronous review.
